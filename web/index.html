Random Dungeon

A verifiable randomness beacon on Solana.

A randomness beacon publishes a fresh random output every epoch and asks the world to trust it. The hard part is not generating random bytes — every operating system can do that. The hard part is convincing a stranger that the bytes were not chosen, and could not have been chosen, by anyone in particular. Manipulability of the last digit is enough to break a fair lottery.

Random Dungeon commits four independent real-world entropy sources (USGS earthquakes, NOAA weather, Bitcoin block hashes, drand) into a canonical manifest, hashes that manifest into a 32-byte seed, feeds the seed through a verifiable random function, and proves the VRF was computed correctly with a Groth16 SNARK. The proof is 256 bytes and verifies on Solana in milliseconds. The byte-for-byte inputs that produced today's output are below.

Four sources of entropy

Each epoch fetches four independent feeds. Each feed is canonicalized to a fixed-layout byte string, hashed with SHA-256, and bound into the manifest. No source's contribution can be silently re-rolled.

And then a zero-knowledge proof

The seed feeds a Verifiable Random Function. We prove the VRF was computed correctly with a Groth16 SNARK. Anyone can verify the proof on-chain in milliseconds without re-running the computation or knowing the secret key.
